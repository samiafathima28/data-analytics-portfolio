THE QUESTION

Problem Statement

Housing market data contains information about sales, regions, property types, prices, and market activity. However, raw housing records can make it difficult to understand how the market is performing across different regions and property categories. This project focuses on transforming housing data into meaningful insights that help identify sales patterns, regional performance, pricing relationships, and differences across house types.

METHODOLOGY

Analytical Approach

The analysis began by preparing and understanding the housing market data and identifying the main dimensions available for analysis. The data was examined across sales, regions, house types, pricing, and other market-related fields to create a structured foundation for analysis.

The next stage focused on analysing housing sales performance across regions and different sales categories. Comparisons were made between regions, prices, and sales activity to identify important patterns and differences in market performance. The relationship between offer prices and purchase prices was also examined to provide a clearer view of pricing behaviour.

The final stage involved transforming the analysis into an interactive Power BI report. DAX was used to support calculations and measures, while the dashboard combined charts, KPIs, and comparisons to communicate the findings clearly. Separate views were used to examine the overall housing market, sales performance, and house-type analysis.
