ABOUT

Hi, I'm Samia Fathima

I enjoy making sense of complex information and finding the story behind the numbers. Data gives me a structured way to understand problems, identify patterns, and turn information into something useful.

My interest in data analytics grew from a curiosity about what drives results and how seemingly small details can reveal something bigger. I became increasingly interested in exploring datasets to understand trends, relationships, and the factors behind business performance.

That curiosity led me to build my skills in Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, and Tableau. I work with data to clean and prepare information, perform analysis, develop dashboards, and communicate insights in a clear and meaningful way.

I believe effective analytical work isn't about making things unnecessarily complicated. It's about asking the right questions, finding what matters, and making the answer easier to understand.
