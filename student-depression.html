Analysis

Analyzing student data to explore patterns associated with academic pressure, financial stress, study habits, sleep duration, study satisfaction, and student well-being.

Power BI | Python | Excel

THE QUESTION

Problem Statement

Student well-being can be influenced by several academic, lifestyle, and financial factors. This project explores student data to understand patterns related to academic pressure, financial stress, study satisfaction, study hours, and sleep duration, using student counts to compare the different categories.

METHODOLOGY

Analytical Approach

The analysis began by preparing the student dataset and identifying the variables relevant to the study. The selected factors included academic pressure, financial stress, study satisfaction, study hours, and sleep duration.

The data was then explored by grouping students across the different categories and calculating student counts. These comparisons made it possible to examine how the distribution of students changes across the selected academic, lifestyle, and financial factors.

The final analysis was presented through Power BI visualisations. Different charts were used for each factor so that student distributions could be compared clearly and the overall findings could be communicated through an interactive dashboard.
